Taxes

by Jeffry R. Fisher

There's more to taxes than just lowering them (although I do think that's a good idea.) There are also matters of fairness and unintended consequences. Here's where I present some thoughts and ideas about how taxes should be calculated and/or collected.

Essays:

  • Assessor Beware

    If the assessor thinks my property is worth so much, then he should be ready to pay that price... really.

  • Tax the States

    Have the federal government and states face off, freeing the populace to contend with just one level of government trying to pick our pockets

  • Stabilizing Social Security * NEW *

    Failing total privatization, we can at least stave off disaster without tripling payroll taxes as the baby boom retires.
