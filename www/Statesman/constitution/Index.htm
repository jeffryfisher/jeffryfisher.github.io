Constitutional Issues, Doctrine and Proposed Amendments

by Jeffry R. Fisher

These are my opinions and ideas (some borrowed) regardingthe US Constitution. How is it (or should it be) interpreted? How could we amend it to strengthen checks and balances?

Essays:

  • Sunset all Federal Laws** NEW **

    Ever noticed how, once enacted, even controversial laws rarely get repealed, even when the government changes hands? We need to translate Congressional inaction into expiration rather than perpetual authority.

Cross Links:

Constitutional issues can crop up in almost any of my topics. Some proposed amendments pro and con elsewhere on my site:
  • Electoral College

    People misunderstand it and its purpose, so they just want to get rid of it (the way we ruined the Senate with the 17th Amendment). We should fix its flaws rather than throwing it away.

  • Repeal the 17th Amendment

    Decentralized government suffered a severe blow when the Senate stopped answering to state legislatures. The formal connection between federal and state legislatures had been a check against the expansion of federal power into matters handled by individual states. The 17th Amendment destroyed it; we should restore it.

Coming Attractions:

  • Presumption of Liberty

    When liberty and federal authority collide, should the burden of proof rest first upon citizens to find explicit Constitutional protection, or should we be assumed to be at liberty until the government proves its explicit authority?

  • 9th Amendment in the Bill of Rights

    The 9th amendment was important enough to be enshrined in the Bill of Rights, but almost nobody in government can give it any useful meaning. If Congress is too stupid to comprehend part of the Bill of Rights, does that justify their ignoring it completely?

  • Civil Asset Forfeiture

    Is Civil Asset Forfeiture an "Excessive Fine"? Being imposed by default, does it violate "innocent until proven guilty"? What about the right to a jury trial in matters exceeding $20? Is there any amendment in the BoR that is not violated by CAF?

  • Is "War Powers" Legislation Illegitimate?

    When Congress passes "war powers legislation" delegating the final war-declaration decision to the president, has it violated the Constitution?
