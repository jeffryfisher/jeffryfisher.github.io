Morality

by Jeffry R. Fisher

In spite of the first amendment, many people try to codify religious doctrine. As a born again Christian and Libertarian, I must come up with artful reconciliations of the apparently conflicting philosophies. I think outside the box, and I rely heavily on the principle that placing my faith in government undermines my faith in God.
