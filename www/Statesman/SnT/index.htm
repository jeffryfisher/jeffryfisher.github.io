Political Strategies and Tactics

by Jeffry R. Fisher

A few out-of-the-box ideas that could change campaigns, voting strategies, and legislation.

Essays:

  • Repeals Committee

    Wouldn't it be nice if at least a few legislators were put in position where the only way to act or get attention was to scrap bad law and relinquish unconstitutional authority?

  • Congressman in Training

    An idea for getting up to speed before running for office so I wouldn't be proposing to take on a new job with no clue how it works.

  • A New Kind of Debate

    Staged debates have barely enough time for meaningless slogans and mostly unchallenged fallacies. Let's use the power of the Internet to conduct a real debate over the course of weeks. With many cycles, candidates could explode their opponents' fallacies to explore issues rationally (Hey, I can dream, can't I?).

  • "Radical"

    Looking at an overused and oft misunderstood propaganda word

  • Escaping from the Two-Party System

    Tired of feeling compelled to choose between the lesser of two evils? There are better electoral systems out there. Even before enacting one, determined voters can liberate each other from two-party tyranny (but be warned, the powers that be have made it illegal in most states).

  • The Myth of the Wasted Vote

    The worst wasted vote is one lending support to a harmful cause. The second worst is the vote not cast. At least voting independent for your true favorite candidate has some benefits, even if s/he doesn't win.
